01

Brand

UltradynamicoUSA · Made in Japan

Useful is not
enough.
It has to be beautiful.

Ultradynamico is an American tire brand made by two old friends and self-described tire fetishists, Ron (Ultraromance) and Pat (Ultratradition). To them the bicycle is where utility meets artistic design, so a tire's ride and its looks matter equally.

After nearly fifty years of combined tread awareness, they had grown numb to garish logos, bizarre tread patterns and overbuilt, lifeless casings. Years of all-terrain riding, a passion for high-end rubber, an eye for design and a dash of third-grade science led them to make their own. The startup funds came from selling some long-forgotten stock.

Design

Tires for the connoisseur.
For people who know their tires.

Tires change the look of a bike more than any other part. Ultradynamico finishes a build with a soft amber skinwall, a quiet label and a geometric tread, and reads the road through a supple, high-TPI casing. Your bicycle is an extension of your personality, and your riding style a window into your soul.

Every model is named after a wine: Cava, the sparkling wine of Catalonia, Brut, the dry sparkling style, Rosé, and Mars, from Marsala, the fortified wine of Sicily. The names are playful. The tires are serious.

TreadThe fuller the glass

From the smoothest, Cava, to the knobbiest, Mars. The four treads deepen in order, like a glass being filled.

CasingSkeleton, heart and soul

The casing is the skeleton and the heart of a tire. Ride quality, liveliness and personality all come from here.

SkinwallMystery casing

The sidewall colour varies slightly from batch to batch. Like wine, the hue can change within the same name.

Founders · Ron & Pat

“We only wanted to make one tire that didn't exist. Even if nobody else wanted them, the two of us would be set for life.”

Ron is better known as Ultraromance, a rider who helped popularise relaxed bikepacking and adventure riding. His workshop is in a 300-year-old barn on a farm in the lower Connecticut River valley, and that barn is also where Ultradynamico tires are warehoused. Most of the other bike parts he designs are released through Crust Bikes.

Pat, known as Ultratradition, is Ron's longtime friend. In the 2000s the two co-founded the road racing team Wooly Mammoth in Austin, Texas, and they have shared a fussy taste in tires ever since.

Work began around 2018, and the first tire, Rosé, arrived the following year. They drew the treads and knobs from scratch, combining what they loved in their favourite tires with rider feedback, and production is handled by Panaracer in Japan.

Ron (Ultraromance), co-founder of Ultradynamico

02

All-road · Gravel

Cava

The speed of a slick,
trust in the corners.
Like a fine Catalan Cava.

Cava is a minimalist tread for riders who want the free speed of a high-volume slick but need a tire that is more predictable in loose corners. Its 3D diamond file tread massages granular terrain for a perceived 20 to 30% more traction than a slick.

A continuous center of delta shapes keeps rolling resistance low, and it is less prone to tread slashes than a true slick. It pairs well in the rear with a knobbier Rosé or Mars up front. The 700C x 33 is a road classics tire for the cobbles and short dirt sectors of Flanders, not a gravel tire.

03

Fast · Dry

Brut

Smooth on pavement,
a little more bite in the dirt.
Between Cava and Rosé.

Brut was dreamed up with fast, dry terrain in mind, and its tread depth lands between Cava and Rosé. The goal was a tire that rolls smoothly on pavement yet bites a little more than Cava in the dirt.

Tightly spaced micro triangles sparkle on asphalt, and once tipped onto singletrack they give confident braking traction and grip. Tread rubber extends down the sidewall for a little added protection without spoiling the ride of the lightest, most supple Race casing. Brut means almost no sugar, yet this is a very sweet tire.

04

Gravel · All-terrain

Rosé

The first tire,
the mainstay of the range.
Pavement and dirt alike.

Rosé was Ultradynamico's first tire and is still the mainstay of the range. It handles pavement and dirt like a skilled musician handles a Stradivarius. A near-continuous center tread drives you forward, and delta knobs deliver braking traction when you re-enter the gravel.

After about 1,000km, a file-pattern undertread hidden beneath the knobs is revealed. This tire actually gets faster with age. Run Rosé front and rear for maximum off-road traction, or swap the rear to Cava to drift the corners a little more.

04

A tire made to pair

Two Rosés for off-road grip, or a Cava in the rear for lively corners. The pairing changes the character.

Rosé · GreyAmber skinwall

Pair it

Rosé up front, Cava in the rear

Ultradynamico tires are designed to pair with each other. Cava has slightly more volume than Rosé, giving it the same effective outside diameter, so running Rosé up front and Cava in the rear leaves the bike's geometry unchanged.

The knobbier front tire steers through corners and braking, while the smoother rear reduces rolling resistance. For rougher roads choose two Rosés, for faster ones two Cavas.

05

ATB · Mountain

Mars

Veer slightly off course,
and you end up
in a place like Mars.

Mars is the knobbiest tread in the range, designed by riders and tuned for ATB performance. A ramped, continuous center delta rolls fast when you pedal and brakes with confidence when you modulate.

Side knobs won't let you down in loose, dry corners, and wide knob spacing sheds mud while leaving artistic patterns in the dust. The name comes from Marsala, the fortified wine of Sicily. With a 26 inch option, it suits classic MTB rebuilds too.

06

Casings

Race · JFF · Rozzo · RobustoSame tread, four characters

The casing is the skeleton of a tire,
its heart and soul.

A tire's ride quality, liveliness and personality all come from its casing. Ultradynamico makes every one of its four treads in four casings. From the lightest, most supple Race to the Robusto that handles anything, choose by your riding style and your roads.

RaceLightest and fastest

The fastest compound with zero sidewall protection. The best ride quality, but no forgiveness for punctures. If you are an aggressive rider who punctures often, consider Rozzo.

Flat protectionNone

JFFEveryday lightness

An accessible everyday tire with lightweight construction at the best price. A slightly thicker casing than Race and a longer-wearing compound. Approachable luxury.

Flat protectionBasic

RozzoSolid at low pressure

Race compound with bead reinforcement and sidewall protection. Burp-free at low pressure. Run Race up front and Rozzo in the rear.

Flat protectionReinforced

RobustoHandles anything

Sidewall protection with a slightly harder tread compound makes this the most durable casing. Good for loaded touring. Available only in the toughest colour imaginable: black.

Flat protectionMost

Story

Austin · Connecticut · JapanTwo friends, one obsession

Tires made
by two friends
fussy about tires.

There was no grand plan. They wanted to make one tire that didn't exist, and figured that even if no one bought it, the two of them could use it for life. That small company now makes four treads in four casings, across more than thirty specs.
